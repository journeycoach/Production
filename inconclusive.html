    What an even split means

    An equal distribution across all three centers usually points to one of two things:

    • Situational range. You adapt your leadership style depending on the context — which is a genuine strength, but it can also mean your default pattern is harder to see from the inside.
    • Insufficient separation. The questions did not create enough contrast to surface your primary center. This can happen when someone leads from a well-practiced professional identity rather than instinct.
    • A genuinely blended profile. Some leaders operate from all three centers in close balance. If that is you, a coaching conversation is the most reliable way to identify which center leads under real pressure.
    What to do next

    A 30-minute assessment call cuts through the ambiguity faster than any assessment can. We will look at specific leadership moments — decisions you have made, feedback you have received, and patterns others have named in you — and identify your center from there. You can also retake the assessment and focus on answering from your first instinct rather than your best judgment.
