What an even split typically means

An equal distribution across all three centers usually points to one of three things:

  • Situational range. You adapt your leadership style depending on context — a genuine strength, but one that can make your default pattern harder to see from the inside.
  • Answered from the ideal, not the instinct. Short assessments can surface a professional identity rather than a primary center. If you were thinking about what a good leader would do rather than what you actually do, the scores will flatten out.
  • A genuinely blended profile. Some leaders operate from all three centers in close balance. When that is true, a brief conversation is the most reliable way to identify which center leads under real pressure.

Two ways to get a clearer picture

Taking the assessment again later, perhaps with a specific high-pressure scenario in mind, often yields a clearer result.
