Privacy Policy

    Effective date: April 4, 2026

    Your Journey Coach ("we," "us," or "our") operates journeycoach.co. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

    Information We Collect

    We only collect information you provide directly through the contact form on this site:

    We do not use tracking cookies, advertising networks, or analytics services that collect personal data. No account creation is required to use this site.

    How We Use Your Information

    Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to:

    We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

    How Your Information Is Stored

    Contact form submissions are stored securely in a private database hosted by Neon (neon.tech), a PostgreSQL cloud provider. This database is accessible only to site administrators and is not publicly exposed.

    Email notifications of new submissions are delivered via Forward Email (forwardemail.net), a privacy-focused email service. The site itself is hosted on Vercel (vercel.com).

    Data Retention

    Contact submissions are retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose they were collected for. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

    Your Rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

    • Access the personal information we hold about you
    • Request correction of inaccurate data
    • Request deletion of your data
    • Withdraw consent at any time

    To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

    Children's Privacy

    This site is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
