Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Your Journey Coach ("we," "us," or "our") operates journeycoach.co. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site, how we use it, the service providers involved, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly through forms and assessments on this site:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Area of interest
  • Your message
  • Hidden Ceiling Assessment responses and result, if you complete the assessment

If you accept optional analytics, we collect page visits, a randomly generated browser identifier, referral information, and campaign parameters such as UTM source, medium, and campaign. This helps us understand which resources and channels are useful. We do not use this identifier to learn your real-world identity.

No account creation is required to use this site.

Analytics and Your Consent

Google Analytics and our first-party visit counter remain disabled unless you choose “Accept analytics.” If you decline, the site continues to work and no analytics identifier is created by our visit counter.

You can change your analytics choice at any time. Changing your preference affects future collection on this browser.

Local and Session Storage

The site uses browser storage for the following purposes:

  • Remembering your analytics choice
  • Saving assessment progress so you can continue after refreshing the page
  • Temporarily carrying your assessment result and scheduling link between result pages
  • Remembering referral and visit information only when analytics is accepted

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through forms and assessments is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Follow up regarding coaching services you expressed interest in
  • Deliver requested assessment results, guides, and email follow-ups
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the site
  • Understand which channels and resources are helping people find the site

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers

We use service providers to operate this site:

  • Vercel hosts the website and server functions.
  • Neon provides the private PostgreSQL database used for contacts, subscribers, assessment results, and site content.
  • Resend sends contact notifications, requested resources, assessment follow-ups, and campaign emails.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile helps protect forms from spam and automated abuse.
  • Google Analytics provides optional usage analytics after consent.
  • Calendly processes scheduling information when you choose to book a conversation.

These providers process information according to their own privacy terms and only for the services they provide to us.

Data Retention

Contact, subscriber, and assessment records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide follow-up services, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time, subject to records we are required to retain.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
